# Store copy — English

## Name (≤ 30)
Aura — a space for two

## Subtitle (App Store, ≤ 30)
Memories, chat and dates

## Short description (Google Play, ≤ 80)
Memories, chat, dates and letters only the two of you can see — every day.

## Full description (≤ 4000)
Aura is a warm, private space made for just the two of you. There are no followers and no public
feed — only two people, joined by an invite, ever see what you share.

Every day
• Send a heartbeat with one tap, or let them know you're thinking of them.
• Answer the daily question — you see each other's answer only after you both reply.
• See how many days you've been together, plus your partner's local time and weather.

Memories
• Keep photos and short videos with their date, place and tags, and scroll back through your timeline.
• Each of you can write your own side of the same memory.
• Albums, search, a map of your places, a yearly recap and a printable PDF book.

Chat
• Text, photos, voice messages, files and locations, with replies, reactions and pins.
• Disappearing messages; anything you write offline is sent once you're back online.

Plans and together
• Suggest a date — your partner accepts or proposes another time. Checklists, budget and reminders.
• 150+ date ideas, a random pick, and a secret swipe deck that only shows the ideas you both liked.
• Anniversaries, birthdays, a bucket list, shared goals, letters delivered on a future date and time capsules.
• A daily mood check-in — you decide each day whether to share it.

Privacy
• Your data is yours: download everything as a ZIP or delete it at any time.
• App lock with a PIN, Face ID or fingerprint, and remote sign-out of your active devices.
• No third-party analytics and no ads. Data travels over TLS.

Aura is free — no payments, subscriptions or in-app purchases. For people aged 18 and over.
